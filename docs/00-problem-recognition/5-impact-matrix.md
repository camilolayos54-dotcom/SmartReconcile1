# Impact Matrix (Deliverable 5)

**Project:** SmartReconcile  
**Document ID:** D5-IMPACT-MATRIX  
**Phase:** 0 — Problem Recognition  

---

## 1. Stakeholder & Organizational Impact Evaluation

| Stakeholder Group | Current Pain Point | Financial / Operational Impact | Urgency Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Finance Operations Teams** | Wasting hours manually mapping PDF/CSV files and cross-referencing bank line items in Excel. | Loss of 15–40 productive hours/week; fatigue-induced operational errors. | **CRITICAL** |
| **CFO / VP of Finance** | Delayed monthly book closures (5–10 days post month-end) and financial leakage. | Direct loss of 0.2%–0.5% GMV; inaccurate cash flow forecasting. | **CRITICAL** |
| **Engineering Teams** | Constantly building and fixing custom bank statement ingestion scripts. | High technical debt; engineers pulled away from core product roadmap. | **HIGH** |
| **External Compliance & Audit** | Inability to verify manual ledger adjustments without clear audit notes. | Audit flags, regulatory risk penalties, and lack of line-item traceability. | **HIGH** |

## 2. Risk of Inaction

If this problem is left unaddressed:
1. **Scalability Ceiling:** Transaction volume growth will linearly increase finance headcount costs.
2. **Compound Leakage:** Uncaptured bank fee errors will accumulate silently over fiscal years.
3. **Audit Failure:** Inability to achieve SOC-1 / SOC-2 type compliance due to unexplainable manual accounting overrides.
