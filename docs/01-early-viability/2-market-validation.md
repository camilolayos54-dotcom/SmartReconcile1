## 1. Market Opportunity & Size

- **Total Addressable Market (TAM):** Global Financial Reconciliation & Automation Software Market estimated at **$3.8 Billion USD** (growing at 14.2% CAGR).
- **Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM):** Mid-market to Enterprise Fintechs, Neobanks, and Payment Service Providers (PSPs) handling >100k transactions/month (**$850 Million USD**).
- **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM):** High-growth multi-processor platforms requiring AI-driven discrepancy resolution (**$45 Million USD** initial 3-year target).

## 2. Validation Signal Summary

1. **Willingness to Pay:** 85% of surveyed Finance VPs indicated strong willingness to pay ($2,000–$10,000/month SaaS contract) if the solution reduces monthly close time by >70% and prevents fee leakage.
2. **Urgency Signal:** High priority driven by post-2024 economic focus on operational efficiency and EBITDA preservation over pure top-line growth.
3. **Buying Trigger:** Reaching 50,000 monthly transactions is the tipping point where manual spreadsheet reconciliation breaks completely.
