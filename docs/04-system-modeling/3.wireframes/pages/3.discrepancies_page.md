# Wireframe Specification: `/discrepancies` (Discrepancy Review & Audit Center)

**UI Route:** `/discrepancies`  
**Injected Functional Modules:** `MOD-AGT` (AI Discrepancy Resolution Engine), `MOD-DET` (Accounting Validation), `MOD-UI` (Executive PDF Report Export).  

---

## 1. Cognitive Analysis & Recommended UX Pattern

- **Diagnosis:** Analysts need to inspect non-matching transaction items ($\Delta \neq 0$), review AI step-by-step mathematical reasoning, approve balancing journal entries with single-click confidence, and export executive PDF reconciliation summaries for external auditors.
- **Primary UX Pattern:** `Master-Detail Inspection Layout (Master Discrepancy List + Side-by-Side Detail Panel)`. Left pane lists pending discrepancy cards sorted by value; right pane displays the side-by-side transaction comparator, AI reasoning card, and primary approval/export controls.

---

## 2. UI Inventory (Atomic Design)

### A. Atoms
- `Heading` (H1 Title: "Discrepancy Review & Audit Center").
- `Button` (Primary Green: "Approve Adjustment", Secondary Red: "Reject & Escalate", Export Button: "Download Signed PDF Report").
- `Badge` (`UNANNOUNCED_FEE` - Amber, `FX_VARIANCE` - Blue, `LOW_CONFIDENCE` - Red).
- `Icon` (Check, X, Sparkles, AlertCircle, ArrowRight, ShieldCheck, FileText, Download).
- `Chip/Tag` (Confidence Score Tag: "98% AI Confidence").
- `Divider/Separator` (Vertical line separating internal ledger record from bank record).

### B. Moléculas
- `DiscrepancyListItem` (Selectable list card with RefID, Delta Amount $\Delta$, Root Cause Badge, Timestamp).
- `TransactionComparatorCard` (Internal Ledger Amount vs. Bank Settlement Amount with highlighted $\Delta$ difference).
- `AIReasoningStepCard` (Sparkles Icon + Step Number + Natural Language Mathematical Explanation).

### C. Organisms
- `DiscrepancyQueueBlock` (Filterable list panel of unmatched items with search input and category pills).
- `SideBySideInspectorBlock` (Master detail container comparing Internal DB Record vs. Bank Statement Line).
- `AIProposalApprovalBlock` (Proposed balancing journal entry table + "Approve Entry" / "Download Executive PDF Report" button container).

---

## 3. Spatial Heuristics & Accessibility (Layout Rules)

1. **Approval Action Dominance (Fitts's Law):**
   - The "Approve Adjustment" primary button must sit at the bottom-right of the inspector pane with a large touch target (`min-h-[48px] px-8 py-3 bg-emerald-600 hover:bg-emerald-700 text-white rounded-lg`).
2. **Executive PDF Export Access:**
   - "Download Signed PDF Report" button sits in top header bar allowing finance leads to download SOC-2 audit compliance reports for the current batch.
3. **High Contrast Highlight:**
   - The discrepancy delta amount must be highlighted using high-contrast text (`text-amber-600 dark:text-amber-400 font-bold`).
